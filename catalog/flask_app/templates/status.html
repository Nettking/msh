MTConnect network discovery

Scan an explicit private IPv4 subnet for MTConnect Agents. MSH reads each Agent's /probe data and identifies machines by UUID, with serial number, Device id, and address as fallbacks. A generic name such as Mazak is never the only identity.

For safety and predictable scan time, only RFC1918 IPv4 ranges of /24 or smaller are accepted. Scan and source changes must be made from localhost on the MSH machine. The scan runs from the MSH Docker network, which is also where the recorder connects.
