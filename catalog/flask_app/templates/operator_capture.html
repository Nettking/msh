Step 1: record the statement

What happens next?

1. Capture

Save the raw statement quickly while you are with the machine or operator.

2. Review later

Open Review Notes and translate the statement into OSL fields such as observation, hypothesis, action, rationale, and trade-off.

3. Reuse

Mark good structured notes as reusable. They can then appear in strategy comparison and SysML export.
