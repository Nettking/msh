How this model works

The state on each sample is applied until the next sample for the same machine. Only intervals from 0.1 to 30 seconds are included. Longer gaps and unknown states are left out of the time-share denominator. Reported execution states are preferred; when execution is missing or unavailable, spindle speed above 100 rpm or load above 1 infers active time.

The what-if keeps the selected observed time fixed and moves the chosen share of non-active time into active time. It is an assumption to explore, not a measured intervention effect.
