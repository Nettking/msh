Assist

Assist is for operator support during monitoring. It does not decide for the operator. It shows possible explanations, safe next steps, risks, and alternatives that the operator can accept, reject, or postpone.

How support cards work

1. Situation

A card starts from a current issue, a common CNC template, or a reusable operator note.

2. Suggestion

The card explains possible causes, a suggested next step, the reason, the risk, and an alternative.

3. Confirmation

The operator records what they did. That response can later become evidence for a reusable strategy.

Support cards

Cards marked as template-based are prompts for thinking. Cards based on reusable notes are stronger because they come from previous operator experience.
