## Historical categories

- [Phases](phases/) — completed phase plans, progress records, designs, and closeouts.
- [Handoffs](handoffs/) — superseded continuation instructions and agent handoffs.
- [Audits](audits/) — repository audits, cleanup manifests, and historical review records.

Historical documents may explain why the system was built a certain way. They may not be treated as the current next task.
